IMPORTANT SOURCE QUALITY
Downloaded source files were public low-quality 24 kHz MP3 PREVIEWS, not the
original 44.1 kHz source MP3 masters. Original downloads require login and were
not obtained. The preview's two channels are sample-for-sample identical, so
mono retains all recorded channel information. Derivatives remain 24 kHz;
converting to WAV does not restore detail lost in the preview encoding.

DERIVATIVES
v12-idle-loop.wav: long preview 3.9-6.9 s, bass-rich timbral bed, 2.88 s cycle.
v12-body-loop.wav: long preview 12.5-16.5 s, denser body timbre, 3.88 s cycle.
v12-rev-event.wav: complete short preview, 5.330 s, non-looping native-pitch event.
The two beds are NOT measured low/mid/high-RPM or load-specific recordings.
The approved short preview contains several rev swells, followed by a final
decay. Throttle-lift handling fades that event; it is not a separately recorded
overrun layer. No high-speed loop is manufactured from the changing rev.

PROCESSING
Identical-channel mono extraction; DC removal; shared -4.450 dB attenuation;
PCM16 quantization. Beds use a 120 ms half-cosine equal-gain seam and a circular
boundary rotation. The full event has a 15 ms entrance fade and an 80 ms exit
fade. No authoring EQ, compression, synthesis, pitch shifting, or time stretching.
Exact source cuts, hashes, peaks, seams, band-energy statistics, limitations and
100 ms event envelope: qa/audio-derivatives.json.
Rebuild: python tests/make_v12_layers.py [path/to/lamborghini-audio-research]
Authoring dependencies: ffmpeg, NumPy, SciPy. They are not runtime dependencies.

Selection and checks use numeric waveform/spectral analysis. No claim is made
of subjective listening validation, voice-free background, true RPM mapping,
or production-master quality. Preview artifacts/background sound may remain.

配布元がV12のMurcielagoと申告した録音の低品質MP3プレビューを使用しています。
原盤ではなく、実際のR34/RB26の録音でもありません。24 kHzの同一左右チャンネルを
モノラル化し、同じ録音から低音のベッドと厚み用のベッドを作成しています。
2つのベッドは音色上の役割であり、実測RPM・負荷に対応する録音ではありません。
承認された短い空吹かし音は全体を自然速度で使用し、高回転ループへ引き伸ばしません。
人による試聴品質、背景音声がないこと、実車音響の再現性は未検証です。
